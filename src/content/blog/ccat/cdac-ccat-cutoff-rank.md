## CDAC C-CAT Cutoff & Rank

One of the most common questions after the C-CAT exam is:

> **"Is my rank good enough to get PG-DAC?"**

Unlike many entrance exams, **C-DAC does not publish an official cutoff rank for every course and centre.** Admissions depend on counselling, seat availability, candidate preferences, and the number of applicants in each admission cycle. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

As a result, the cutoffs you see online are **estimated from previous counselling rounds** and should be treated as a guideline rather than a guarantee.

---

## Approximate Rank Ranges by Course

| Course | Competitive Rank |
|---|---:|
| PG-DAC | Under **1,500** |
| PG-DBDA | Under **2,000** |
| PG-AI (formerly PG-DAI) | Under **2,000** |
| PG-ITISS | Under **2,500** |
| PG-DESD | Under **3,000** |
| Other PG Certificate Courses | Varies depending on centre |

These are broad trends gathered from previous admission cycles and counselling data. Highly sought-after centres such as **C-DAC ACTS Pune** generally require much better ranks than newer or smaller centres. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

## Which Rank Is Considered Good?

| Rank | Admission Chances |
|---|---|
| **1 – 300** | Excellent. Almost every centre and course is available. |
| **300 – 800** | Very good. High chance for top PG-DAC centres. |
| **800 – 1,500** | Good. PG-DAC remains achievable at many centres. |
| **1,500 – 2,500** | Good options for PG-DBDA, PG-AI, PG-ITISS and several PG-DAC centres. |
| **2,500 – 4,000** | Limited choices but still good opportunities depending on counselling rounds. |
| **Above 4,000** | Focus on later counselling rounds and a wider range of centres and courses. |

Remember that every counselling round changes the effective cutoff as candidates accept, reject, or upgrade their seats.

---

## Why Do Cutoffs Change Every Batch?

Several factors influence the final closing ranks:

- Number of candidates appearing for C-CAT.
- Difficulty level of the examination.
- Number of seats offered in each course.
- Popularity of individual C-DAC centres.
- Candidate preference order during counselling.
- Number of counselling rounds conducted.

Because these factors vary every admission cycle, **there is no fixed cutoff that repeats every year.** :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Centre Matters More Than You Think

A rank that easily secures admission to one centre may not be sufficient for another.

For example:

- C-DAC ACTS Pune usually has the highest demand.
- Bengaluru and Hyderabad centres are also highly competitive.
- Newer centres often remain available until later counselling rounds.

Always consider both **course** and **centre** while filling your preferences.

---

## How Counselling Affects Your Admission

Admissions are allocated according to:

1. Your C-CAT rank.
2. The order of course and centre preferences you submit.
3. Seat availability at the time of counselling.

A better rank gives you more flexibility, but even candidates with moderate ranks often receive good seats by filling their preferences wisely. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

---

## Don't Chase Only PG-DAC

Many students focus exclusively on PG-DAC, but several other programmes also offer excellent placement opportunities, including:

- PG-DBDA
- PG-AI
- PG-ITISS
- PG-ASSD
- PG-DESD

Choose a programme that matches your interests rather than selecting solely based on popularity.

---

## Key Takeaways

- There is **no official C-DAC cutoff list** for every course and centre.
- Previous-year cutoffs are only estimates based on counselling trends.
- A rank below **1,500** is generally competitive for PG-DAC.
- Better ranks provide more centre choices, but counselling strategy also matters.
- Fill as many realistic preferences as possible during counselling instead of relying on a single option.
