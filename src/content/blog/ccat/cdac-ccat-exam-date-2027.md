## CDAC C-CAT Exam Date 2027 (February Batch)

If you're planning to join a **C-DAC PG Certificate Programme in February 2027**, your journey begins with the **January 2027 C-CAT examination**.

At the time of writing, **C-DAC has not officially announced the February 2027 admission schedule**. However, C-CAT follows a consistent admission cycle every year, so we can estimate the important dates based on previous batches. ## Typical Admission Timeline

The February admission cycle generally follows this sequence:

1. Online registration opens.
2. Fill the application form and pay the examination fee.
3. Download your admit card.
4. Appear for the C-CAT examination.
5. Check your C-CAT rank.
6. Fill your preferred courses and centres during counselling.
7. Participate in seat allocation rounds.
8. Report to the allotted centre and begin classes.

The entire admission process usually takes around **two to three months** from registration to course commencement. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## Which Exam Should February 2027 Aspirants Write?

Many first-time applicants get confused about this.

- **January C-CAT → Admission to the February batch**
- **July C-CAT → Admission to the August batch**

If your goal is to join **PG-DAC, PG-DBDA, PG-AI, PG-DESD, or any other C-DAC programme in February 2027**, you should apply for the **January 2027 C-CAT**. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## When Should You Start Preparing?

For the best chance of securing a top rank, aim to begin preparation **4–6 months before the exam**.

A suggested timeline is:

| Time Before Exam | Focus |
|---|---|
| August – September 2026 | Learn C Programming and Aptitude fundamentals |
| October – November 2026 | Complete Data Structures, OOP, Operating Systems and Computer Networks |
| December 2026 | Solve previous papers and mock tests |
| January 2027 | Revision and full-length mock exams |

Starting early gives you enough time to strengthen weak areas instead of relying only on last-minute revision.
