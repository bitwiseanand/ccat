## CDAC C-CAT Syllabus

The **CDAC Computerized Common Admission Test (C-CAT)** covers a wide range of subjects from aptitude to core computer science and electronics. Before starting your preparation, it's important to understand **what topics are actually included in the syllabus**.

While C-DAC publishes the subjects tested in each section, it **does not release an official chapter-wise weightage**. This guide combines the official syllabus with commonly observed topic distributions from previous C-CAT exams to help you prioritize your preparation.

---

## C-CAT Exam Sections

The examination consists of three independent sections.

| Section | Subjects |
|---|---|
| Section A | English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, Computer Fundamentals |
| Section B | C Programming, Data Structures, C++, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, AI & Big Data |
| Section C | Computer Architecture, Digital Electronics, Microprocessors |

Depending on the admission category of your desired course, you may need to attempt one, two, or all three sections.

# Topic Weightage (Observed Trends)

> **Note:** C-DAC does **not** publish official chapter-wise weightage. The following estimates are based on previous admission cycles.

| Subject | Approximate Questions |
|---|---:|
| C Programming | 15–18 |
| Operating Systems + Computer Networks | 10–12 |
| Artificial Intelligence + Big Data | 9–12 |
| Data Structures & Algorithms | 9–12 |
| C++ & OOP | 8–10 |

The exact distribution changes from batch to batch.

# How to Use This Syllabus

A good study sequence is:

1. Understand the syllabus.
2. Learn the concepts.
3. Solve chapter-wise MCQs.
4. Revise short notes.
5. Attempt full-length mock tests.

Avoid trying to study every subject simultaneously.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Section A is common for all candidates.
- Section B is the most important section for software-related programmes.
- Section C is required mainly for electronics and embedded programmes.
- C-DAC publishes the subjects but **does not release official chapter-wise weightage**.
- C Programming consistently carries the highest weightage in previous exams.
- Focus on understanding concepts before solving MCQs.
